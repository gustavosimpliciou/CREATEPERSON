---
name: Dreamloft3D Chibi asset license
description: Licensing restriction for the user-supplied free Chibi Base Mesh pack.
---

The supplied Dreamloft3D Chibi Base Mesh Pack license allows use and modification as part of a larger final project, but prohibits sharing, reselling, or redistributing its meshes as-is or modified, including in GLB, OBJ, FBX, and STL formats. The free version is marked for evaluation and limited use.

**Why:** The Character Studio is intended to export customized 3D models, which could otherwise redistribute source-pack geometry in user downloads.

**How to apply:** Do not include these meshes in standalone library downloads or user exports unless the license is clarified to cover downstream redistribution. Procedural or independently licensed geometry is the safer default.
